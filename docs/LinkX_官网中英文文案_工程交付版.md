**中文**  星连资本成立于 2021 年，专注人工智能与下一代智能技术的早期投资。在这一轮智能浪潮成为市场共识之前，我们就已对 AI 的长期潜力形成坚定判断，并持续投入研究与资本。我们重点支持研究型创业者（researcher-founders），相信深厚的科研积累与独到的技术洞察，能够成为独特产品和优秀公司的起点。

我们的投资覆盖六个方向：基础模型与学习范式、芯片、AI 基础设施、AI 原生应用、具身智能、科学智能。

我们在研究与创业的早期阶段就与团队建立联系，通过长期资本、孵化支持和产业资源，帮助研究成果转化为产品、走向市场。我们寻找能够拓展智能边界、重塑产业格局的下一代公司。

**English**  Founded in 2021, LinkX Capital invests early in AI and the technologies shaping the next generation of intelligence. We built conviction in AI’s long-term potential before the current wave gained broad market recognition, and have backed it with sustained research and investment. We support researcher-founders, believing that deep research expertise and distinctive technical insight can lay the foundations for differentiated products and exceptional companies.

Our investments span six areas: Foundation Models & Learning, Chips, AI Infrastructure, AI-Native Applications, Embodied AI, and AI for Science.

We work with teams from the early stages of research and company formation, combining long-term capital, incubation support and industry connections to help turn research into products and bring them to market. We look for the next generation of companies that expand the frontiers of intelligence and reshape industries.

模型的规模、训练数据和计算效率仍在影响智能能力的上限。推理、多模态和新训练方法不断发展，但技术路线尚未定型。对研究团队而言，重要的是找到能够带来可验证能力提升的问题，并持续迭代。

#### 让已有能力进入真实任务

#### Put existing capability to work

Strong benchmarks do not automatically translate into reliable work. Agents must understand goals, use tools, respond to feedback and adapt across settings. Better training environments, evaluation methods and real-world data can help close that gap.

Application teams should start with specific work and turn model capability into dependable products. Healthcare, research, manufacturing and physical systems each impose different constraints.
